Read this before you rely on a number. Catalogue figures are typical published values for models sold or imported into Sri Lanka, and they only ever prefill the form — the saved car is whatever you typed. Charging sockets in particular vary by source market, so check your own charge port rather than trusting the catalogue. Real battery capacity drops with age. The charge curve is a general model; your car, its battery temperature and the charger's own thermal limits all move it. Rates are yours to enter and nothing is assumed about who supplies your electricity.
